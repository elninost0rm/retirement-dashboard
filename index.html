Model notes & assumptions
Figures are in today's dollars and grow at (1 + nominal return) / (1 + inflation) − 1. Service years are calculated from your federal start year. Pension paths: VERA at the VERA age/year with the minimum service (full nominal, eroding to the normal age, then COLA); MRA+30 at the MRA age with the minimum service (COLA from the start); otherwise deferred to the normal pension age and eroded from your leave age. The multiplier is 1.0% per service year, 1.1% at 20+ years. The spouse is modeled as 3 years older; spouse Social Security starts at your age 59 and yours at 62. Health costs are zero while anyone is working. Retiring on an immediate annuity (VERA, MRA+30, normal) keeps FEHB: the FEHB premium applies from then on, with Medicare added as each of you turns 65. Without FEHB (or on a deferred annuity) the ACA premium applies instead, halved when only one of you is on it. Medicare is a household amount, half for one person from your age 62 (when your spouse turns 65) and full from your 65. Withdrawals come from brokerage first, then TSP once penalty-free access begins at 59½. The mortgage payoff year and month are inputs, and the projection runs through age 95.
